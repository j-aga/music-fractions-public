👂 Aural Lab

The four Preliminary aural tests. At Preliminary each test is played once — so listen hard the first time.

Tests 1–4 are the four Preliminary aural tests. Every example is generated fresh each time to the specification AMEB publishes — so he practises the skill, not a memorised list.

Here is the tune you just heard:

The tune you heard, and the beat you were clapping:

Black notes are the tune — some long, some short, some between the beats. The beat row is evenly spaced: that is what you clap. The you row is where your claps actually landed — green on the beat, red off it. Each dashed line shows where that clap belonged, so the gap is how early or late you were.

The examiner plays it through, then again one note at a time, waiting for you to sing each one.
